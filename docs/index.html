01 What it is

A practical operating layer for company spend.

Purchasing rarely happens in a straight line. A request needs a budget, an approval may depend on authority limits, goods have to be received, and invoices must agree with what was ordered. SpendSync keeps those related decisions and records together.

It is designed for internal requesters, approvers, purchasing teams, finance teams, and vendors. Each group works with the same lifecycle from a different, appropriate view.

02 A typical example

Equipping a new team

Ordering laptops for new starters.

A department needs ten laptops before new colleagues join. Rather than handling the request across email, spreadsheets, and separate finance records, the purchase can move through one visible process.

  1. 1

    The requester creates a requisition, selects the devices from the catalog, and assigns the relevant cost centre.

  2. 2

    The approver reviews the request against their authority limit and the available budget.

  3. 3

    The purchasing team turns the approved request into a purchase order for the chosen supplier.

  4. 4

    When the delivery arrives, the receiving record confirms what was received and whether anything needs attention.

  5. 5

    Finance compares the supplier invoice with the order and goods receipt before it is included in a payment batch.

03 The working flow

The application follows the procure-to-pay lifecycle, with budget and approval checks where they are needed.

  1. 01

    Request

    A requester creates a purchase requisition, using catalog data where it is available.

  2. 02

    Review

    Budget availability and delegation-of-authority rules shape the approval path.

  3. 03

    Order

    An approved request can move into a purchase order and supplier-facing workflow.

  4. 04

    Receive

    Goods receipts and inspection records establish what was actually delivered.

  5. 05

    Match & pay

    Invoices are checked against the order and receipt before entering a payment batch.

04 Capabilities

Useful context throughout the purchasing process.

BUDGETS

Commitment-aware planning

Budget pools, reservations, transfers, and enforcement modes keep intended spend visible before it becomes an invoice.

GOVERNANCE

Contextual access

Tenant boundaries, roles, facilities, legal entities, and approval limits are part of the operating model.

MATCHING

Evidence before payment

The three-way matching engine compares purchase order, goods receipt, and supplier invoice data, exposing discrepancies for review.

VISIBILITY

Records with a timeline

Audit logs, analytics, and spend intelligence make it easier to understand how money moved and why.

05 How it is built

One deployable application, organized around business domains.

SpendSync uses a modular-monolith architecture. Domain modules keep their own responsibilities while sharing the application’s security, event, persistence, and observability foundations. This reduces operational overhead without treating the procurement lifecycle as one large, indistinct feature.
